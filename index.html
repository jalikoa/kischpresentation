Glass Waste to Tiles & Construction Materials

The Glass Problem

  • Hazardous: Sharp fragments pose safety risks
  • Hard to Recycle: Requires high-temp furnaces; not viable locally
  • Landfill Accumulation: Non-biodegradable; persists for centuries

End Products

Decorative Tiles
Floor Tiles
Wall Panels
Composites
Architectural Surfaces

Why It Matters

  • Underexplored Market: Few local players in recycled glass products
  • High-Margin Output: Premium pricing for architectural finishes
  • Construction Demand: Growing need for sustainable building materials
  • Green Architecture: Aligns with LEED & modern eco-design standards

Benefits
